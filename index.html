About me

I'm a Data Engineer and Architect from Shizuoka, Japan, with a foundation in software engineering and an MSc in Computing Science (Distinction) from Oxford Brookes University. I started out building BI dashboards, then moved into full-stack and infrastructure engineering, and now design enterprise-scale data platforms and AI-driven automation agents. I bridge cloud infrastructure (AWS) with full-stack development (Java, Next.js), and bring advanced proficiency in the Microsoft Fabric ecosystem. I'm a certified Scrum Master, focused on delivering scalable, high-performance solutions that align data engineering with agile delivery.
